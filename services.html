Frequently Asked Questions

How long can I borrow a book?

Standard members can borrow up to 3 books for 14 days. Collector members can borrow up to 5 books for 30 days.

Can I reserve a reading room?

Yes, members can reserve our private study and reading rooms up to a week in advance through the member dashboard.

Do you accept book donations?

We gladly accept donations of books in good condition. Rare or antique books can also be appraised by our curation team.
